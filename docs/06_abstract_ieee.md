**Title:** Energy Detective: An Evidence-Grounded, Constrained-Planning AI Agent for Building Electricity Anomaly Investigation

**Abstract—** Buildings accounted for nearly 45% of the growth in global electricity demand in 2025, yet turning whole-building meter data into actionable investigations still depends on manual expert interpretation. Fixed thresholds cannot separate weather, operational change, and data-quality artifacts, and recent studies report that large language models (LLMs) remain unreliable when asked to judge numerical time series directly. This paper proposes Energy Detective, a domain AI agent that lets facility managers request an investigation of a specified building and period in natural language. The agent replays hourly electricity, weather, and metadata from the Building Data Genome 2 dataset to emulate an IoT stream. All numerical work is deterministic: a quality gate checks units, time axes, and coverage; hour-of-week median baselines are fitted on a training window, thresholds are calibrated on a separate window, and same-direction alerts are merged into events on a held-out test window. The LLM is restricted to structured request parsing, whitelisted tool planning under a fixed call budget, and one observation-based selection of precondition-checked domain rules. Every numerical claim in the report must cite an evidence identifier and match its building, period, unit, and value; otherwise a deterministic template is used. The planned evaluation compares global and hour-of-week baselines, measures sensitivity to injected anomalies, and compares a fixed report, a fixed pipeline with an LLM, and the constrained agent on ten registered cases using task success, fact traceability, and unsupported-claim counts. No experiments have been conducted yet; the expected outcome is a reproducible investigation prototype that does not claim fault diagnosis or verified energy savings.

**Index Terms—** Anomaly detection, building energy management, evidence grounding, large language model agents, measurement and verification, smart meters, time series analysis, tool use.
